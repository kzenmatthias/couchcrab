# Code of Conduct

Couchcrab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be welcoming and kind, to contributors and to the creators whose work this project is about.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination or sexualised content. Many fans are young; keep everything family-friendly.
- Don't share anyone's private information.
- Don't use Couchcrab to spread rumours or drama about creators.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/kzenmatthias/couchcrab/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainers through the repository. Reports are handled confidentially.
